MONTHLY REPORT FOR OCTOBER

Department: Sales and Repairs
Company: Gigabyte
Reporting Period: Saturday, 7 November 2026

1. Introduction

This report provides a summary of the company's activities and performance for the month of October, covering laptop repairs, laptop sales, and accessories recorded during the period.

2. Summary of Activities

A. Laptop Repairs

A total of 81 laptops were brought in for repairs during the month.

The breakdown is as follows:

• 35 laptops: Had hardware issues. The faults were fixed, and the laptops were picked up by their owners.

• 12 laptops: Had software issues. The necessary software repairs were completed.

• 22 laptops: Are still with the company, pending confirmation of their repair status or the reason for the delay.

• 12 laptops: the customer left with those laptops based on financial issues or the the system can't be fixed and some of them promise to come back this month but we haven't see them yet

B. Laptop Sales

A total of 45 laptops were sold during the month of November.

C. Accessories

A total of 43 accessories were recorded during the month.

• 12 accessories: Were recorded for office use and as gifts to customers.

• 31 accessories: Were recorded for other purposes, including sales.

3. Observations

• The company received a considerable number of laptops for repairs during the month.

• Hardware-related faults accounted for 35 completed repairs.

• Software-related repairs were also handled, with 12 laptops successfully serviced.

• Some laptops are still pending, and their repair status needs to be confirmed.

• The company recorded 45 laptop sales, showing active sales performance.

• Accessories were also recorded, with some allocated for office use and customer gifts.

4. Recommendations

• Proper Repair Documentation: All laptops received for repairs should be properly documented, including the customer's details, nature of the fault, and repair status.

• Regular Follow-up: Customers whose laptops are still pending should be contacted to confirm the status of their repairs and any possible delays.

• Timely Repairs: The technical team should work towards completing repairs within reasonable timeframes to improve customer satisfaction.

• Improved Sales Strategies: The company should continue to improve its marketing and customer service strategies to increase laptop sales.

• Accessories Monitoring: Proper records should be maintained for accessories sold, used in the office, or given to customers as gifts.

• Accurate Record-Keeping: Monthly records should clearly account for every laptop and accessory received, repaired, sold, used, or distributed.

5. Conclusion

In conclusion, the month of November recorded activities in laptop repairs, laptop sales, and accessories management. A total of 81 laptops were received for repairs, 45 laptops were sold, and 43 accessories were recorded. Although some repair cases are still pending confirmation, the company continued to carry out its daily business activities. Improved documentation, customer follow-up, and timely service delivery will help enhance the company's overall performance in the coming months.
